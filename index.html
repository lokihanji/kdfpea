About The Association

Empowering fertilizer processors and agri-entrepreneurs.

KD Fertilizer Processor-Entrepreneurs Association is a community-driven organization committed to producing quality fertilizer products while promoting sustainable farming practices and local entrepreneurship.

Through collaboration, skills development, and responsible production, the association helps strengthen agricultural productivity and livelihood opportunities within the community.

✓ Promotes organic and eco-friendly fertilizer processing
✓ Supports local farmers and small agri-business owners
✓ Encourages sustainable waste-to-resource practices
